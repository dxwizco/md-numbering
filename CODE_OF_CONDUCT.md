# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in the Markdown Numbering project.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, users, and community members to interact professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Focusing on the issue or contribution rather than the individual.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Respecting project maintainers, contributors, and users.
- Using inclusive and professional language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or contributions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the **[DXWIZ Contact page](https://dxwiz.com/contact)**.

Please provide enough information to help us understand the situation and, where appropriate, include links or other relevant context.

Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, including warnings, removal of comments or contributions, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project participation.

The maintainers may also decline to take action when a report does not fall within the scope of this Code of Conduct.
